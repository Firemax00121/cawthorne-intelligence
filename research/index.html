Why these subjects belong together

In April 2026, Reuters reported that Nigeria's NNPC had shipped approximately 950,000 barrels of Cawthorne crude to the Netherlands from a floating storage and offloading vessel serving OML 18. That is a documented physical-market event. It does not, on its own, reveal the transaction's price differential, identify a repeatable series of buyers or establish a daily Cawthorne benchmark.

Independent price assessments require their own market evidence and publication rules. Platts' February 2026 launch of FOB Nigeria assessments for Nembe and Okwuibome illustrates the importance of defined cargo specifications and market conventions. Separately, a refinery's willingness to buy a grade depends on its delivered economics and operating requirements—not merely the grade's country of origin.

The useful research question is therefore not just whether a Nigerian cargo has moved, but whether repeated, comparable observations make its pricing more transparent to producers, traders, refiners and other market participants.
