NETBACK ENGINE · DRAFT
REFINERY VALUE = PRODUCT YIELD VALUE − PROCESSING COST − FREIGHT − LOSSES − QUALITY PENALTIES
FAIR FOB CAWTHORNE = MAX(REFINERY NETBACKS) − BUYER MARGIN / RISK BUFFER

The platform's moat is not merely reporting a barrel price. It estimates who should pay the most for that barrel and why, creating buyer-targeting and negotiation intelligence.
